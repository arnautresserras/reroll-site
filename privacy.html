Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Reroll Forever is a free iPhone app made by an individual developer, Arnau Tresserras. This policy explains what the app collects and why. In short: no account, no personal data, and only anonymous usage events.

What the app does not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number.
  • No location, contacts, photos or other content from your device.
  • No advertising identifier. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Anonymous usage events

To see which features people use, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, an analytics service, using its servers in the European Union. The events are:

  • entry_selected: whether you opened the quiz or the name generator.
  • quiz_started and quiz_completed: which quiz you took, and the class and race it suggested.
  • name_rerolled: the race you picked, and whether the first name was locked.
  • name_favorited: the race of the name you saved.
  • card_shared: which share card you shared.

Each event also carries technical details that the analytics library adds, such as the app version, the iOS version, the device model and the language setting. Events are tied to a random identifier the app creates on first launch. It is not linked to your identity, and deleting the app removes it.

The names you generate, your favorites and your settings stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Photos

If you choose Save Image from the share sheet, iOS asks for permission to add the card to your Photos library. The app can only add images. It cannot see or read your photos.

Sharing and selling

Usage events are not sold, and are not shared with anyone for advertising. They are used only to understand and improve the app.

Retention

Usage events are kept for as long as the analytics plan retains them, at most one year, and then deleted.

Your choices

You can stop all collection by deleting the app. To ask about the data or request its deletion, email arnautresserras@gmail.com. Because events are anonymous, it helps to include roughly when you used the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
