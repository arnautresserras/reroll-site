Reroll Forever

Decide your next character for WoW Forever.

Get it on the App Store

Free for iPhone. No account, no ads.

Find your class and race

Eight quick questions about how you like to play. Get a class with the reasons behind it, then pick a race yourself or let a short race quiz decide. Every race shows its racial traits.

Roll a name

First name and surname for every Forever race, Skyborne included. Lock the half you like and reroll the other. Save favorites and copy them straight into the game.

Share your pick

Turn your result or your new name into a card and send it to your guild.
