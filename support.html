Support

Questions, bug reports or a wrong rule in the app? Email arnautresserras@gmail.com. Replies usually come within a few days.

Is this an official Blizzard app?

No. Reroll Forever is an unofficial fan app, made by a fan. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment.

Where do the races, classes and racial traits come from?

They are summarised from Blizzard's published World of Warcraft: Forever material, with racial trait details checked against public spell tooltips. Content updates arrive without an App Store update.

The app suggested a race and class that can't be combined

Please email which race and class it showed. Combinations are fixed in a content update as soon as they are confirmed.

A generated name is rejected in game

Please email the name and your race. Names follow the published naming rules, but the game also blocks names already taken on your realm, which the app cannot know.

Does the app need an account or internet?

No account. Everything works offline. Anonymous usage events are sent when a connection is available; see the privacy policy.
